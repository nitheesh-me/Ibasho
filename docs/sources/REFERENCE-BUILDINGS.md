# Reference buildings: sources

Where the information for each sample building comes from. Every fact is from a public source or marked estimated or assumed in the building's notes (`packages/fixtures/src/buildings/<id>.notes.json`). No private documents are shipped; downloaded source files stay local.

Buildings of interest (Lloyds Technology Centre, Qualcomm, Vanguard) are leased offices in towers owned by others. No public floor plans were found for any of them, so their floors are estimated from the OpenStreetMap outline and the developers' stated areas.

## Sattva Knowledge City, Octave Block: home of the Lloyds Technology Centre (`gb7`)

**Occupier:** Lloyds Technology Centre (Lloyds Banking Group). Floor 10, Octave Block, per the company address. Opened in November 2023 with about 600 specialists in technology, data and cyber security.

**What is measured, estimated or assumed:**

- footprint: OpenStreetMap outline (about 8,900 m2) as three rectangles. OpenStreetMap does not name the blocks, so this outline is our best match for the Octave Block (assumed)
- storeys: Estimated: ground floor and 17 office floors at 4.2 m, 3 basement levels; the tenant floor (10) is known
- floorPlate: Estimated: no public floor plan was found; about 1.7 million sq ft above ground, in line with one of the five large buildings
- cores: Estimated: three service cores
- wwr: Assumed: 50% glazing
- rating: Announced: IGBC Platinum for the park, per the developer

**Sources:**

- Lloyds Technology Centre, corporate details: 10th Floor, Unit 3, Plot No. 2, Octave Block, Phase 1 of Cyberabad Knowledge City, Raidurg, Hyderabad 500081. <https://lloydstechnologycentre.com/corporate-details>
- Lloyds Banking Group opens technology centre operations in Hyderabad (November 2023). <https://lloydstechnologycentre.com/posts/lloyds-banking-group-opens-technology-centre-operations-in-hyderabad-2>
- IBS Intelligence: Lloyds Banking Group to invest in a new technology centre in Knowledge City, about 600 roles. <https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/lloyds-banking-group-to-invest-in-new-technology-centre-in-india>
- Sattva Group: Sattva Knowledge City, 30 acres in five parcels (Parcel 03 is Octave), five large buildings, about 9.7 million sq ft, IGBC Platinum. <https://sattvagroup.com/commercial/sattva-knowledge-city/>
- OpenStreetMap way 852979202, a Sattva Knowledge City building outline (ODbL). <https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/852979202>

## KRC Commerzone Raidurg, host of the Qualcomm Hyderabad campus (`gb5`)

**Occupier:** Qualcomm India (GCC). The whole building, fully leased to Qualcomm on a lease of about 12 years (Mindspace REIT, 2025). Earlier reports mention 17 floors and about 1.6 million sq ft.

**What is measured, estimated or assumed:**

- footprint: Measured: OpenStreetMap outline (about 12,100 m2) as three rectangles; it may include podium edges
- storeys: Estimated: ground floor and 19 office floors at 4.5 m, 3 basement levels. OpenStreetMap gives 22 levels and 100 m; the architect describes a 20 storey superstructure
- floorPlate: Estimated: no public floor plan was found. The outline on every floor gives about 2.6 million sq ft above ground, close to the stated leasable area
- cores: Estimated: two central service cores, one per tower
- wwr: Assumed: 55% glazed curtain wall

**Sources:**

- OpenStreetMap way 1133460476 "Qualcomm Commerzone": 22 levels, height 100 m (ODbL). <https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1133460476>
- KRC Commerzone, Hyderabad: 20 storey superstructure, two conjoined towers on an L-shaped footprint, central cores with high and low zone lift banks, 9 acre site, 4.5 acre podium, 2.63 million sq ft leasable. <https://world-architects.com/en/projects/view/krc-commerzone-hyderabad>
- K Raheja Corp: Mindspace REIT to acquire 1.8 million sq ft Commerzone Raidurg (Sustain Properties), fully leased to a Fortune 500 GCC, completed 2023. <https://krahejacorp.com/newsroom/mindspace-reit-to-acquire-18-million-sq-ft-it-park-in-hyderabad-for-rs-2038-cr>

## Vindhya Bhavan, IIIT Hyderabad (`gb1`)

**What is measured, estimated or assumed:**

- layout: Measured: rectangles fitted to the OpenStreetMap outline and its four courtyards; wing rooms from the campus floor plans
- names: read: room functions from the plan sheets (RB 3.2), functions only, no personal names
- floorToFloor: assumed 3.9 m
- origin: OSM outer ring centroid in the city-hyderabad frame; rotation 41.7 degrees
- cellar: partial under C4 and C5; the NE face is exposed (sloping site) with ventilators
- shading: Estimated: an RCC chajja over every window band, 750 mm on the SW and NW facades and 600 mm on the NE and SE facades, 150 mm above the window head. The plan sheets show no elevations, so depths follow typical Hyderabad institutional practice (450 to 900 mm). No fins or jaalis are assumed.

**Sources:**

- IIIT Hyderabad estate office, Vindhya Bhavan floor plans (Dec 2022). <https://intranet.iiit.ac.in/offices/static/files/Vindhya_bhavan.pdf>
- OpenStreetMap relation 2829427 (ODbL). <https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2829427>
- Ibasho brief RB 3. <docs/research/v3/reference-buildings.md>

**Unknowns:**

- window sizes and glazing (WWR 14% assumed from tag counts W1, W2, V2)
- wall and roof build-ups (RCC frame, masonry infill likely)
- chajja depths and continuity (estimated, not drawn; fins and jaalis not assumed)
- permit date (built before 2014 TSECBC; edition unknown)
- roof PV
- lift and stair cores are modelled as rooms, not cores
